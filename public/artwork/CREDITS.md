**B2 and B3 replace B1.** These come from the same "50 Views of Rome" volume the Carceri
search led me to, and they solve the tone problem directly: B2 is *architecturally* the exact
structural grammar Part 4 describes — coffered vault, receding colonnade, single vanishing
point — in a warm, scholarly register, not a dungeon. B3 is a classic "warm ruin" view,
monumental without being dark. Both at 2514×2302, comfortably above what we need for
desktop/mobile art-directed crops.

## Selection needed before I process anything

**Anatomical** — I'd propose **A1** (the walking écorché) as the primary Beat 2 plate: it's
dynamic, gestural, and it's an actual plate style from the Fabrica tradition the brief calls
out by name. **A2** (skeleton at the classical tomb) is the standout secondary choice — it's
the one image that is *literally* both categories at once, anatomy posed against architecture,
which is the whole thesis of this redesign. I'd use it for About. A4–A6 are the three plates
already sourced and processed in the current build; they fit the brief's allowances (Albinus
is explicitly named) and I'd keep them in rotation for Countdown/Register rather than resource
new plates for every slot, to stay inside the 6–8 budget.

**Architectural** — B1 (Piranesi's Carceri) is the strongest match I found for "structural
grammar," but I want to flag a tone concern before going further: Carceri plates are
*prisons* — chains, a torture wheel, an imaginary dungeon. The brief's register is "scholarly,
warm, monumental," explicitly not gothic/dark. This plate is visually spectacular but reads
closer to gothic than monumental. I have not yet found a *Vedute di Roma* (Piranesi's Roman
ruin views — the gentler, "warm ruins" side of his catalogue) or Palladio/Serlio plate at
comparable resolution; I can keep searching if the group wants a genuine architectural plate,
or we can lean entirely on the brief's own instruction that arches/bays/coffers are procedural
SVG hairlines, not photographic, and skip a dedicated architectural photo plate altogether.

Final budget proposal pending your call: **A1, A2, A4, A5, A6** (five anatomical plates,
reusing three already on disk) **+ B1 only if you want the Piranesi despite the tone, or a
replacement once I find a warmer architectural candidate.**
